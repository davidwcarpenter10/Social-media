# Brand Voice & Guardrails

## Who we are
HonorCraft Custom Works LLC — veteran-owned laser engraving & custom fabrication, Piketon, OH.
Run by David and Lisa. Shopify: honorcraftlw.com · Etsy shop.
Name usage: "HonorCraft" or "HonorCraft Custom Works". Do not add new uses of "Laser Works".

## Voice
- Warm, proud, plain-spoken. A craftsman talking to a neighbor, not an ad agency.
- Specific over hype: name the wood, the size, the personalization, the moment it's for.
- Short sentences. Active verbs. One idea per line on short-form platforms.
- Humor is fine; sarcasm and politics are not.

## Audiences
1. Military / veteran families (retirements, promotions, memorials, unit gifts)
2. Civilian gift buyers (weddings, anniversaries, holidays, Father's/Mother's Day)
3. Local Southern Ohio (community, events, pickup)
4. Business / organizations (bulk, awards, signage)

## Content pillars (target mix)
| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Product Spotlight | 30% | finished piece, options, price |
| Process | 30% | laser running, sanding, reveal |
| Gift Moment | 20% | the occasion, the reaction, who it's for |
| Behind the Shop | 10% | David & Lisa, the shop, the dog, the mess |
| Community | 10% | local events, customer features (with permission) |

## Guardrails (QA blocks on any violation)
1. No uniformed endorsement: don't show David in uniform or use rank/unit to imply DoD or Guard endorsement. "Veteran-owned" is fine.
2. No official military seals/insignia as post graphics; none on products unless licensed or customer-supplied with permission.
3. No "official / approved / issued" government wording.
4. Truthful claims: "Made in USA" only if all or virtually all of the product qualifies; otherwise "Engraved in Ohio". No fake reviews, fake scarcity or false urgency.
5. Customer names/photos only with documented permission.
6. Price and options must match the live listing.
7. AI-media flags set correctly on every platform.
8. Keep The Ohio Six Foundation separate from sales posts.
9. Never use AI imagery that misrepresents the actual product.
