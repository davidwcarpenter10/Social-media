## 2. Shot list for David (phone, at the laser)

**Phone setup:** hold the phone vertically, 1080p or 4K at 30 fps, lens wiped, exposure locked on the product
(tap and hold). Use the lens shroud or light for the laser, and wear safety glasses if needed. Film each shot about 2× longer than the times listed below so the editor has room to cut.
Keep the shop's natural sound (laser hum, fan); music is added later.

**Rules for every shot:** hands only. No David in uniform, and no rank, unit or military patches in the frame.
No real customer names, photos or orders on screen. Use sample text such as "Baby's First Christmas 2026",
"Forever in our hearts" or "Buddy", or text David and Lisa own. Use **no** military seals, branch emblems or insignia on any piece you film
(see section 4 for the Memorial Ornament).
